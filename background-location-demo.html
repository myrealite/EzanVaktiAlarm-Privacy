EzanVaktiAlarm — Background Location

Demonstration for Google Play review of the optional Smart Location Quiet Mode (Akıllı Konum Sessizliği), which monitors user-selected saved places while the app is not in the foreground. The recording shows the enabled tracking screen and its ongoing Android notification.

This recording does not demonstrate the initial Android permission grant sequence. It is supporting footage of the tracking function, not a substitute for permission disclosure or policy requirements.
